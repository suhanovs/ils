# AboutILS V2 — phased roadmap

Each phase ends with a usable release and a pause for review. Future scope may change after feedback. V1 remains available at `/`; V2 is published separately at `/v2/`.

- [x] **Phase 1 — Historical hurricane explorer.** Publish a responsive Hurricane Ian map with NOAA NHC historical track, cumulative 34/50/64-knot wind-swath thresholds, timeline playback, attribution, and limitations. Verify both V2 and V1 live.
- [ ] **Phase 2 — Today's exposure and market.** Add latest compatible Census GEOID geography and housing data, then build a synthetic Florida exposure book informed by available insurer market-share and county-level regulatory data. Explain data provenance and approximation.
- [ ] **Phase 3 — From hurricane to investor.** Apply a transparent illustrative damage model and insurance and reinsurance terms to show exposure → loss → layer → investor capital. Validate with hand-calculated examples.
- [ ] **Phase 4 — Portfolios, replay and market cycles.** Add diversified cedents and deals, historical season replay, trapped collateral, capital survival, and explainable market-pricing dynamics.
- [ ] **Phase 5 — Monte Carlo and outcomes.** Run up to 10,000 seeded scenarios using the same event and financial pipeline; present 3-, 5-, and 10-year distributions, downside, survival and comparisons without rendering every storm track.

## Principles

Historical tracks are observed/reconstructed datasets; wind swaths represent estimated cumulative potential extent, not parcel-level measurements. Exposure, loss, contracts and returns remain modeled and must be labeled. No result or return is guaranteed. Use consistent shared events across cedents to preserve geographic correlation. Phase completion requires live verification and review.
