# Florida housing data and illustration

The lazy-loaded `public/data/fl-tracts.bin` is gzip-compressed GeoJSON (the `.bin` suffix prevents a server automatically decoding it as HTTP content encoding). It contains 5,122 Florida tract polygons, 10,256,470 estimated housing units, and 90% margins of error. Sources: 2025 Census cartographic boundary 1:500,000 tract file; 2020–2024 ACS 5-year B25001 total housing-unit estimate and margin of error. Tract GEOID joins, not an assertion of identical historical borders. 38 ACS-only GEOIDs have zero estimated housing units and are absent from cartographic geography. The estimate is not current property, policy, replacement value, or loss data. Cartographic boundaries are generalized and unsuitable for precise spatial relationships; no storm intersection, property locations, or loss are calculated.

Rebuild with Python 3 and `pyshp==3.1.8`: `python3 scripts/build_tracts.py /path/to/cache`. Official source URLs are in the script, which caches downloads and writes deterministic gzip. Validate via `npm test`. The sources may change; preserve/check input hashes if exact historical reproducibility is required. This extract derives from US Census public data; Census is the original source, calculations are ours.

The allocation uses fictional names and assumed 50%, 30%, 20% weights, apportioned with largest remainder to total estimated housing units. It is **not** actual insurance exposure or a count of policies. It includes owner, renter and vacant units; no uncertainty propagation is performed. The weights are not OIR data. Although [Florida OIR reports](https://www.floir.gov/tools-and-data/residential-market-share-reports) provide statewide company aggregates, reuse rights have not been confirmed; no OIR figures or company names are included. Statewide shares do not establish local distribution. Partial public county records are not a complete market and are not used.

Sources: [Census ACS](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs), [Census cartographic boundary limitations](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/technical-documentation/naming-convention/cartographic-boundary-file.html), [Census open data](https://www.census.gov/topics/research/research-transparency-public-access/open-data.html), [OIR reports](https://www.floir.gov/tools-and-data/residential-market-share-reports).
